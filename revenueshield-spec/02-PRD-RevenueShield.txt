# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
**Project Name:** RevenueShield (LeadGuard)
**Category:** Micro-SaaS / Conversion Rate Optimization & Lead Protection
**Target Market:** India & Global (Solo Businesses, Clinics, Salons, Digital Agencies, Freelancers)
**Budget:** ₹0 Initial Cost (Serverless & Free-Tier Architecture)

---

## 1. Executive Summary
RevenueShield ek automated Conversion & Lead-Loss Protection tool hai. Yeh local businesses aur agencies ki websites/social bios ko scan karke broken WhatsApp links, invalid phone numbers, broken Google review links, aur dead contact forms ko detect karta hai. Yeh technical errors ko "Financial Loss (₹)" me convert karke dikhata hai aur users ko 24/7 automated monitoring aur white-label audit reports bechta hai.

---

## 2. Target Audience & Personas
1. **Solo Business Owners (Doctors, Salons, Real Estate Agents):**
   - *Pain Point:* Website par WhatsApp button kharab hone se daily 5-10 leads miss ho rahi hain.
   - *Goal:* 24/7 protection taaki ek bhi client na chhoote.
2. **Digital Marketing Agencies & Freelancers:**
   - *Pain Point:* Naye clients ko pitch karne ke liye audit tools bohot mehenge hain (Ahrefs/Semrush $100+/mo).
   - *Goal:* 1-click white-label PDF audit reports generate karke local clients ko redesign/SEO pitch karna.

---

## 3. Core Value Proposition & Psychology
- **Zero Friction Entry:** Bina kisi login ke instant 10-second scan.
- **Loss Aversion Trigger:** "Aap har mahine ₹15,000 - ₹30,000 ke leads kho rahe hain" metrics.
- **The Blurred Paywall (80/20 Rule):** 1 problem free me reveal hoti hai, baaki 3 critical problems blurred rehti hain (Pay to unlock).
- **Free Watchdog Hook:** 24-hour free Telegram/WhatsApp auto-monitor dekar user ka contact number capture karna.

---

## 4. Feature Roadmap & Specifications

### Phase 1: Free Viral Scanner (MVP)
- **Instant URL Analyzer:** Scrapes page HTML, extracts all `wa.me`, `tel:`, `maps.google`, `instagram.com` links.
- **Phone & Link Validator:** Checks for missing country code (+91), digits < 10, invalid URL parameters, HTTP 404/500 errors.
- **Financial Loss Calculator:** Algorithm calculates estimated monthly loss based on standard traffic assumptions.
- **Locked/Blurred Results Card:** Blurs 3 deep issues with a ₹29 instant unlock CTA.

### Phase 2: Lead Capture & Automated Watchdog
- **24-Hour Trial Protection:** Capture user's WhatsApp / Telegram with zero friction.
- **Telegram Bot Alert Engine:** Triggers instant message whenever a monitored link breaks.
- **Automated Expiring Follow-up:** 24 ghante baad alert: "Trial expired. 23 visitors came yesterday. Upgrade to Pro @ ₹199/mo."

### Phase 3: Agency & Monetization Suite
- **1-Click White-Label PDF Generator:** Client ka audit report with Agency's custom logo & branding.
- **Razorpay Subscription Integration:** Recurring monthly automated billing.
