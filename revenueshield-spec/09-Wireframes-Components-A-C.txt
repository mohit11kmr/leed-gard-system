### Component B: Real-Time Scanning Loader
When user clicks "Scan", do not show a boring spinner. Show an engaging sequential terminal log:
- `[✓] Fetching HTML & Meta Tags...`
- `[✓] Validating WhatsApp CTA & Country Codes...`
- `[!] Scanning Contact Forms & Endpoints...`
- `[!] Calculating Estimated Monthly Lead Loss...`
*(Total duration: ~5 to 8 seconds to build suspense and curiosity).*
