## 1. Color Tokens & Visual Psychology

| Token | Hex | Tailwind Class | Psychological Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Background** | `#020617` | `bg-slate-950` | Professional, modern dark SaaS aesthetic |
| **Card Surface** | `#0f172a` | `bg-slate-900` | High-contrast container for critical alerts |
| **Danger / Loss** | `#ef4444` | `text-red-500` / `bg-red-600` | Urgency, immediate panic & loss awareness |
| **Locked Paywall** | `#f59e0b` | `text-amber-400` / `border-amber-500/40` | Curiosity gap & premium value perception |
| **Verified / Safe** | `#10b981` | `text-emerald-400` / `bg-emerald-500/20` | Relief & working status confirmation |
